## Product Overview
**One-liner:** I build AI agents and automations that take repetitive manual work off small and mid-sized ops teams.
**What it does:** Solo Spark LLC designs and builds custom AI agents, workflow automations and LLM integrations. The systems connect the tools a business already uses and handle the copy-paste, triage, data entry and follow-up that eat an ops team's week.
**Product category:** AI automation consultancy / n8n automation consultant / AI agent developer.
**Product type:** Service (custom build, founder-led, fully remote).
**Business model:** Pay as you go. Work is scoped before it starts and paid for as it is done. No published prices. Starts with a free 15-minute intro call.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Small and mid-sized businesses across the US with repetitive, manual back-office or customer-facing work.
**Decision-makers:** Ops managers, office managers, COOs, owners.
**Primary use case:** Replacing repetitive manual work (data entry, routing, follow-ups, document handling, reporting) with reliable automations and AI agents.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get hours back from work that follows the same steps every time.
- Cut errors from retyping data between systems.
- Reply to customers and leads faster, including after hours.
**Use cases:**
- Lead intake and routing
- Invoice and document processing
- Support triage
- Reporting
- Appointment reminders
- AI phone agents (taking calls and orders)

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Skilled people spend their days on repetitive manual work: moving data between tools, sorting requests, chasing follow-ups, building the same report every week.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Off-the-shelf tools don't fit their exact process, so gaps get filled by hand.
- Agencies put a salesperson and project manager between the client and the person building.
- Hiring an in-house engineer is expensive for a small team.
- DIY no-code setups break quietly and nobody owns them.
**What it costs them:** Staff hours, slow replies, missed leads, errors from retyping.
**Emotional tension:** "We're too busy doing the work to fix the work." Worry that AI is hype, or that a system will break and nobody will know how to fix it.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Automation agencies — client rarely talks to the person building; more layers.
**Secondary:** Off-the-shelf SaaS / Zapier-style DIY — doesn't fit the exact process; owned by nobody.
**Indirect:** Hiring more admin staff or an in-house engineer — costly; doesn't remove the repetitive work.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Founder-led: you work directly with the engineer who builds your system.
- Specialist in n8n, plus custom code (Python, Node.js, FastAPI, PostgreSQL) when n8n isn't enough.
- Builds real AI agents (Claude and OpenAI APIs, RAG, MCP/tool calling), voice agents (Twilio).
- Real builds to show: AI phone-ordering agent (prototype for a local Italian restaurant, not launched), research-score-personalize-send pipeline.
**How we do it differently:** Start with one process, build it, hand it over working, explain it in plain English.
**Why that's better:** Nothing lost between sales and engineering. Faster answers. One person accountable.
**Why customers choose us:** Direct access, plain English, pay-as-you-go.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "AI will make mistakes with our customers." | I design for the process: clear rules, human review where it matters, logs of what the system did. |
| "You're one person. What if you're unavailable?" | You get a documented system on tools you own (n8n, your accounts), not a black box. |
| "We're not technical." | That's who I build for. You describe the process; I handle the tech and explain it plainly. |
| "What will it cost?" | Pay as you go, scoped before work starts. We talk numbers after the 15-minute intro call. |

**Anti-persona:** Companies wanting a large in-house platform team, enterprise procurement-heavy projects, or someone wanting a generic chatbot without a real process behind it.
